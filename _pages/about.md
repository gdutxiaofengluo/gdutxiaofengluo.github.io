I am a Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at [Guangdong University of Technology](https://www.gdut.edu.cn/), China, supervised by [Prof. Zhaoshui He](https://teacher.gdut.edu.cn/hezhaoshui/en/index.htm) and [Prof. Jiawen Kang](https://teacher.gdut.edu.cn/kangjiawen/zh_CN/index.htm). I received my B.Eng. degree from Guangdong University of Technology and was subsequently admitted to its postgraduate program through exam-free recommendation, where I completed my master's-stage training before continuing to doctoral study. I was also a visiting student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, working with [Prof. Dusit Niyato](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/dniyato/), and at Singapore University of Technology and Design, working with [Prof. Zehui Xiong](https://sites.google.com/view/zehuixiong).

My research interests include Internet of Vehicles, Security and Privacy, Network Economics, and Metaverse-enabled Networked Systems. My recent work focuses on privacy-preserving mechanisms for Internet of Agents (IoA) and next-generation mobile edge systems.
