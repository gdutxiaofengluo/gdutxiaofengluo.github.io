About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at Guangdong University of Technology, supervised by Prof. Zhaoshui He and Prof. Jiawen Kang. Previously, I received my master's training at Guangdong University of Technology and visited Prof. Dusit Niyato's group at Nanyang Technological University and Prof. Zehui Xiong's group at Singapore University of Technology and Design.

My research interests include Internet of Vehicles, security and privacy, network economics, and metaverse-enabled networked systems. I am particularly interested in privacy-preserving mechanisms, pseudonym management, incentive design, and intelligent digital-twin services for vehicular and next-generation mobile networks.
